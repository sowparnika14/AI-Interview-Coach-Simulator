# 🤖 AI Interview Coach & Simulator

### Practice Smarter. Perform Better. Get Interview-Ready!

## 📌 About the Project

AI Interview Coach & Simulator is an AI-powered web application
that conducts mock interviews and provides detailed feedback
to help students and job seekers improve their interview skills.

The platform creates an interactive interview experience where
users can practice answering interview questions and receive
feedback on their responses.

It aims to help candidates identify their strengths, understand
their weaknesses, and improve their performance before attending
real interviews.

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Many students and job seekers struggle to perform well in interviews
due to a lack of practice, limited access to personalized feedback,
and difficulty identifying their weaknesses.

Traditional preparation methods often do not provide an interactive
interview experience or detailed feedback on individual answers.

Our solution is an AI-powered mock interview platform that conducts
simulated interviews and provides detailed feedback to help users
understand their performance, identify areas for improvement,
and prepare more confidently for real-world interviews.

## ✨ Key Features

- 🤖 AI-Powered Mock Interviews
- 💬 Interactive Interview Question-and-Answer Sessions
- 📊 Detailed Feedback on User Responses
- 🎯 Identification of Strengths and Weaknesses
- 💡 Suggestions for Improving Answers
- 🚀 Interview Practice to Build Confidence
